FREE PIXEL STATUS AURAS & BUFFS
8 animated pixel-art status effects with matching HUD icons. Free.
From Mira Forge. https://miraforge.itch.io
Full version (16 statuses, 5 sizes, framed icons, Godot .tres, Aseprite-format
JSON, 12 palettes): https://miraforge.itch.io/pixel-status-auras-and-buffs-complete

WHAT'S INSIDE
  8 statuses: Burn, Poison, Freeze, Stun, Heal, Haste, Strength Up, Weaken.
  Each has a character overlay in 3 parts: start (plays once), loop, end
  (plays once) = 24 overlay animations at 32 and 64 px, 12 fps, plus 8
  looping HUD icons at 32 and 64 px. Every size is rendered natively.

FILES
  statuses/NN_name/SxS/ and statuses/NN_name/icons/SxS/
    *_sheet.png, frames/..., *.gif     (sheet, single frames, preview)
  pack.json, CATALOG.txt

ANCHORS
  In a 64x64 cell, align local coordinate (31.5, 57) with the character's
  feet (centre between the feet, at the sole line; pixel-centre coordinates).
  Same point at the other sizes: (15.5, 28.25) at 32, (23.5, 42.625) at 48,
  (47.5, 85.75) at 96, (63.5, 114.5) at 128. The cell keeps a 6 px margin (at 64) on
  every side.
  Effects are drawn independently of any body shape, so they work over any
  sprite. Placement = where the effect is meant to sit; Bounds = the actual
  occupied pixels over all start/loop/end frames at 64 px (x0,y0 - x1,y1,
  inclusive). Single frames cover less than the full bounds.
  Burn         Bounds 21,36 - 42,56
               Placement: lower body; flame tongues rise from the feet towards mid-torso
  Poison       Bounds 19,38 - 43,57
               Placement: lower body; bubbles rise from the feet to the hips, drips fall from the hips
  Freeze       Bounds 12,9 - 51,57
               Placement: feet and sides, freestanding; crystal clusters at the feet and at hip height on both sides, flakes falling
  Stun         Bounds 14,8 - 49,16
               Placement: above the head; 3 stars circling in a band above the head
  Heal         Bounds 10,16 - 52,56
               Placement: around the body; plus signs and sparkles rise on both sides of and across the body
  Haste        Bounds 11,16 - 51,46
               Placement: around the body; speed streaks trail behind (left), chevrons point forward (right)
  Strength Up  Bounds 9,13 - 54,54
               Placement: around the body; arrows rise on both sides with glints
  Weaken       Bounds 9,9 - 54,47
               Placement: around the body; arrows sink on both sides and centre
